# DisneyOS Product Vision

> **The intelligent Disney trip companion.**

---

# Mission

DisneyOS exists to simplify the Disney park experience.

Instead of forcing guests to navigate multiple screens inside My Disney Experience, DisneyOS surfaces the right information at the right time with as few taps as possible.

DisneyOS is not intended to replace Disney's official services. It is designed to complement them by organizing information, reducing friction, and presenting guests with actionable recommendations.

---

# Vision

Imagine opening one application that immediately answers the questions every Disney guest asks throughout the day.

- Where should I go next?
- What should I ride now?
- When is my next Lightning Lane?
- How long until my dining reservation?
- Is it going to rain?
- Should I mobile order lunch?
- Which park should I hop to?
- Which ride has the shortest wait?
- Where are my PhotoPass photos?

Instead of searching for answers, DisneyOS brings them together into one intelligent dashboard.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Reduce Taps

Every feature should reduce the number of taps required to accomplish a task.

If DisneyOS cannot make something easier than My Disney Experience, it should not exist.

---

## Context First

Information should be presented based on what matters **right now**, not buried inside menus.

DisneyOS should always know:

- Current Park
- Current Time
- Upcoming Reservations
- Current Weather
- Current Wait Times
- User Preferences

---

## One Glance

The most important information should be visible without navigating through multiple pages.

Users should understand their day within seconds.

---

## Beautiful Simplicity

The interface should feel modern, calm, and intentional.

Design inspiration:

- Apple
- visionOS
- iOS Widgets
- Dynamic Island
- Home Assistant dashboards

---

## Respect Official Services

DisneyOS enhances the Disney experience.

It does not attempt to replace Disney's official applications or services.

Where possible, DisneyOS should launch official Disney experiences rather than duplicate sensitive functionality.

---

# Product Goals

DisneyOS should eventually provide:

## Park Information

- Wait Times
- Park Hours
- Entertainment
- Dining
- Character Locations
- Transportation
- Maps

---

## Trip Management

- Lightning Lane
- Dining Reservations
- Resort Information
- Virtual Queue
- Park Reservations
- Daily Itinerary

---

## Intelligence

DisneyOS should provide recommendations rather than simply displaying information.

Examples:

- Ride Haunted Mansion now.
- Mobile order lunch.
- Move toward Frontierland.
- Wait before riding Space Mountain.
- Rain expected in 25 minutes.

---

## Automation

DisneyOS should integrate naturally with:

- Apple Shortcuts
- Siri
- Home Screen Widgets
- Live Activities
- Home Assistant

---

# Architecture Philosophy

DisneyOS is built using modular components.

Every feature should function independently while integrating into a unified dashboard.

Future additions should require minimal architectural changes.

---

# Long-Term Vision

DisneyOS should feel less like an application and more like an intelligent park companion.

Instead of asking users to search for information, DisneyOS should anticipate their needs and surface the right information before they ask.

The goal is simple:

**Spend less time looking at your phone and more time enjoying Disney.**
