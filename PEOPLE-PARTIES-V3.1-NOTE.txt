DisneyOS People & Parties v3.1 — External Adult Email Approval

Live workflow:
1. Requester finds an adult already connected to DisneyOS Planner who is not a verified DisneyOS member.
2. DisneyOS creates the specific People relationship request.
3. DisneyOS sends the target adult a six-digit approval code and a DisneyOS approval-page link.
4. The target adult opens the link and enters the code themselves.
5. Approval activates only that requester → target relationship in member_people.
6. Managed guests are NOT approved by this action.
7. This does not create a DisneyOS membership for the target adult.

Existing pending email-approval requests gain Send/Resend Approval Email in My People.
