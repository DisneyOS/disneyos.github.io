Find Someone

Search for the adult using their Disney account information. DisneyOS will determine how they need to approve your request.

If they use DisneyOS

They approve your request inside their own DisneyOS account. No email code is needed.

If they are new to DisneyOS Planner

DisneyOS Planner sends a Disney Family & Friends invitation. Accepting that Disney invitation approves the request.

If they are already connected to Planner but do not use DisneyOS

They must approve this specific request using a new code sent to their Disney-verified email.

Managed guests are not searched independently. Add their managing adult first, then expand that adult in My People to request their managed guests.
